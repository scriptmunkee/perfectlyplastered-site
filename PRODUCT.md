## Stack

## Users

- **Homeowners in and around Harrogate** who need walls or ceilings made good: a full room or house skimmed, an artex ceiling removed or covered, a ceiling repaired after a leak or burst pipe, a media wall built. Usually searching on a phone, often after something has gone wrong or before redecorating.
- **Landlords and letting agents** who need reliable repairs and turnarounds between tenants.

Not targeted: builders subcontracting, commercial fit-outs.

## Product Purpose

The website for Perfectly Plastered, Nick's plastering business. Its job is to win local plastering enquiries by phone, WhatsApp or quote form, from people searching "plasterer [town]" and service searches in the area. Success is enquiries for the services below, from the towns below.

## Positioning

Brand first, Nick named: Perfectly Plastered leads and Nick is introduced as the owner. What customers say in their own words, across more than 50 five-star Google reviews on two profiles, is the position:

- the finish: "makes even the roughest of walls look brand new"; walls left ready to paint
- Nick himself: friendly, polite, well spoken, knows his work inside out
- clean and tidy: protects the house and clears up after
- turns up on time, and has worked a Saturday to finish
- fair pricing (the site still shows no prices)
- customers come back for second and third jobs

The name makes the promise; the reviews prove it. No competitor can truthfully copy Nick's review record.

## Product Principles

1. Proof before promise: reviews and real job photos carry the persuasion, not adjectives.
2. Local first: every page answers "do you work where I live?" in its first screen.
3. Contact without friction: call, WhatsApp or a short form, all thumb-reachable on a phone.
4. Plain and honest: no prices, no claims the record doesn't support, nothing a customer could catch out.
5. The finish is the product: the site should feel as clean and exact as a freshly skimmed wall.
